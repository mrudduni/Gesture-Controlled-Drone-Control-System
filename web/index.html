Custom gestures

Teach any hand pose you like. Record ~30 samples per gesture (move your hand around a little while recording), pick an action, then switch to Custom at the top. The starter set comes from other people's hands; recording your own samples on top makes it more accurate for you. Saved in this browser only.

Every trained model runs on the same hand crop. Accuracy is measured on held-out images; HaGRID = people none of the models trained on. *The original CNN trained on all of your captures, so its “your captures” score is flattering. How each model works →
