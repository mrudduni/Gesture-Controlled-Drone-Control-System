Four ways to read a hand

Every mode starts the same way: MediaPipe finds the hand and its 21 joints. The three CNNs then look at a picture of the hand. Custom skips pictures and compares the joint positions to examples you recorded.

Architectures

Each row is a layer; the right-hand columns are the output shape (height × width × channels) and the number of learned weights. Colours: input augmentation convolution pooling dense / classifier other

①

Original CNN

Your first model, from the original cnn for grayscale.py

  • Flatten → Dense(128) turns a 26×26×128 map into one long vector: 11.1M of the 11.2M weights are in that single layer. That's a lot of capacity for 600 photos, so it memorises.
  • The skeleton lines drawn on its training photos became a cue it relies on. The app redraws them the same way so it gets the input it expects.
  • The old augmentation wrote copies into the dataset folders, which leaked into validation and inflated its score.
②

New CNN

VGG-style, trained from scratch on RGB crops

  • Stride-2 stem halves the image straight away, about 4× cheaper with no loss for a hand crop.
  • Pairs of 3×3 convs see a 5×5 area with fewer weights; channels double as resolution halves.
  • BatchNorm keeps training stable under heavy colour augmentation.
  • Global average pooling instead of Flatten: no weights, ignores where a feature is, and 9× smaller than ①.
③

MobileNetV2

Pretrained on ImageNet, fine-tuned on hands

  • Inverted residual blocks: expand channels 6× with a 1×1 conv, filter each channel with a cheap depthwise 3×3, then squeeze back. That's most of the accuracy of a big CNN at a fraction of the compute.
  • Transfer learning: edge, texture and shape detectors learned from 1.3M ImageNet photos carry over to hands, so it needs less hand data.
  • Two-phase training: first only the new head (backbone frozen), then the top 40 layers are fine-tuned at a 10× lower learning rate. BatchNorm stays frozen throughout.
★

Custom gestures

Nearest-neighbour on hand landmarks, running in your browser

  • Normalised joints: positions relative to the wrist, divided by hand size. The same pose matches whether your hand is near, far, left or right in the frame, but pointing left and pointing right stay different.
  • k = 5 nearest samples vote, closer ones counting more.
  • “Unknown” threshold (0.25): if nothing you recorded is close, it does nothing instead of guessing. Calibrated on HaGRID, where same-gesture distances are mostly < 0.17 and different gestures mostly > 0.25.
  • No training step, so new gestures work the moment they're recorded.

What happens after a prediction (all modes)

  1. Majority vote over the last 7 frames, so one bad frame can't flip the gesture.
  2. Confidence ≥ 70%, averaged over those frames.
  3. Held for 0.4 s before it acts.
  4. The gesture's action (editable in the Gestures tab) moves the drone: continuously for movement, once for take off / land.
